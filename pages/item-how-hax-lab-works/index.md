HAX Lab is one part of a loop. A course teaches web development on real HAX problems, student clubs bring their own passions, the lab gives a few students a full term on one project, faculty fund work that solves their own problems, and graduates come back as mentors. Each part feeds the next.

A term in the lab
-----------------

*   **One project you own.** You pick it with the team from real needs in the [unified issue queue](https://github.com/haxtheweb/issues) and see it through to a release.
*   **Real review.** Your pull requests are reviewed like anyone else's. Feedback is the point.
*   **Open office hours, three times a week.** They're open to anyone, and they now draw contributors who were never in the lab.
*   **Public credit.** With your permission, your work is posted on LinkedIn with you tagged, so employers see what you shipped.
