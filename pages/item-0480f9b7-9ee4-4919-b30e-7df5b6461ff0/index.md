Want to join HAX Lab or ask how it works? Here is how to reach us.

*   **Chat:** the [HAX Discord](https://discord.gg/aCGxmRHEJP) is the quickest way to reach us.
*   **Bugs and ideas:** open an issue in [haxtheweb/issues](https://github.com/haxtheweb/issues), the one queue for every part of HAX.
*   **Email:** [hax@psu.edu](mailto:hax@psu.edu)
*   **Security:** report a vulnerability privately through [GitHub security advisories](https://github.com/haxtheweb/issues/security/advisories).

Open office hours run online Monday, Wednesday and Friday, noon to 4 p.m. Eastern, and in person too. Ask in the HAX Discord to join.
