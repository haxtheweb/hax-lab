Want to join HAX Lab or ask how it works? Here is how to reach us.

  • Chat: the HAX Discord is the quickest way to reach us.
  • Bugs and ideas: open an issue in haxtheweb/issues, the one queue for every part of HAX.
  • Email: hax@psu.edu
  • Security: report a vulnerability privately through GitHub security advisories.

Open office hours run online Monday, Wednesday and Friday, noon to 4 p.m. Eastern, and in person too. Ask in the HAX Discord to join.
