Every color, gap and font size in HAX comes from a token: a named CSS variable like --ddd-spacing-4. Change what a token means and every element that uses it changes with it, the same trick as your --card-radius, at the scale of thousands of elements.

  • Read and watch: about 20 minutes
  • Do: about 1 hour
  • You make: your second card restyled with DDD tokens
  • You need: your Project 1b pen
  • Support level: medium

Read and look

  • DDD documentation: open the color, spacing and typography sections and skim. DDD stands for Design, Develop, Deliver. It began as a student independent study and now styles all of HAX.
  • Design tokens (Material Design): another big system explaining the same idea.

One page, three systems

HAX themes can swap which design system their tokens point to. These are the same quiz and checklist from this course, with nothing changed but the design system. Look at heading color and weight, spacing, and accents.

HAX also bridges Carbon, Fluent, Primer, Spectrum, USWDS, Tailwind, shadcn/ui, Bootstrap and Web Awesome. You don't need them now. You need to know the idea: content stays the same, decisions swap.

Play: the token quest

Each idea will find you and ask a question. Answer right to unlock its house.

Do

In the DDD docs, find the spacing scale, the corner radius sizes and two colors you like. Copy their token names and values into your pen's :root, for example --ddd-spacing-4: 16px;. In chapter 4 you'll get the real thing from npm; this is practice with the same names.

Replace your own spacing, radius and color values with DDD tokens. Your variables can point at tokens: --card-radius: var(--ddd-radius-md);

Turn on dark mode on your computer. Does your card still read? DDD colors are written with light-dark() so one rule covers both. Fix anything you can't read.

Open two other design systems, for example PatternFly and Carbon. Find their card. What do they decide that DDD also decides?

Stuck? Ask your study buddy

Use AI to explain, not to do the work for you. Paste your own code or error with the prompt, read the answer, then close the chat and type the fix yourself. If you can't explain the fix out loud, you don't own it yet.
